<summary><strong>Customer Churn Analysis</strong></summary>

<br/>

An end-to-end telecom analytics project developed to identify churn patterns, quantify revenue risk and support targeted customer-retention decisions.

| Category | Details |
|:---|:---|
| **Stack** | Python, Pandas, NumPy, MySQL, Matplotlib, Seaborn |
| **Scale** | Analysis of more than 2,000 telecom customer records |
| **Performance** | More than 20 optimized SQL queries for segmentation, KPI reporting and cohort analysis |
| **Security** | Analytical workflow designed around structured local datasets without exposing customer identities |

| **Repository** | [Explore GitHub Repositories](https://github.com/afsalahamed444-design?tab=repositories) |

### Engineering Overview

This project combines Python-based exploratory analysis with SQL-driven business intelligence to evaluate customer churn across a telecom dataset.

Pandas and NumPy were used for cleaning, transforming and analyzing more than 2,000 customer records. More than 20 MySQL queries supported customer segmentation, cohort analysis, churn-trend evaluation, KPI reporting and revenue-impact assessment.

A seven-chart analytical dashboard visualized high-risk customer groups, churn patterns and potential revenue loss. Based on the findings, targeted retention strategies were proposed with the objective of reducing churn by approximately 15–20%.

**Key Findings**

- 24.9% overall customer churn rate
- Approximately $35K in monthly revenue exposed to churn risk
- High-risk customer segments identified through behavioral analysis
- Revenue-impact assessment supported by SQL-based KPI reporting
- Retention recommendations designed for measurable business outcomes

<summary><strong>Sales Performance Dashboard</strong></summary>

<br/>

A business-intelligence project that transforms multi-region retail transaction data into executive-level revenue, profitability and growth insights.

| Category | Details |
|:---|:---|
| **Stack** | Excel, Power Query, PivotTables, MySQL, Power BI, DAX |
| **Scale** | More than 9,500 transactions across four regions and 25 products |
| **Performance** | Seven SQL queries and reusable DAX measures for automated KPI analysis |
| **Security** | Structured reporting workflow using validated and cleaned transactional data |
| **Impact** | Tracked ₹29.6Cr revenue, 36.7% profit margin and 15% year-over-year growth |
| **Repository** | [Explore GitHub Repositories](https://github.com/afsalahamed444-design?tab=repositories) |

### Engineering Overview

The Sales Performance Dashboard provides a consolidated view of business performance across products, regions and reporting periods.

Excel and Power Query were used to clean, transform and prepare more than 9,500 sales transactions. PivotTables, SUMIFS and COUNTIFS supported initial validation and KPI analysis.

Seven MySQL queries handled extraction, aggregation, regional analysis, revenue trends and top-product reporting. Power BI measures—including **YoY Growth %**, **YTD Revenue** and **RANKX**—enabled interactive business reporting.

The final analysis identified East India as the lowest-performing region and supported a recommendation to expand distributor coverage in the region.

**Key Findings**

- ₹29.6Cr total revenue analyzed
- 36.7% overall profit margin
- 15% year-over-year growth
- Four geographic regions evaluated
- Twenty-five products compared
- East India identified as the lowest-performing region
- Distributor expansion proposed as a regional growth strategy
