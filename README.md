<summary><strong>📊 Sales Performance Dashboard</strong></summary>

<br/>

A business-intelligence project translating multi-region retail transaction data into executive-level revenue, profitability, and growth insight.

| Category | Details |
|:---|:---|
| **Stack** | Excel, Power Query, PivotTables, MySQL, Power BI, DAX |
| **Scale** | 9,500+ transactions across 4 regions and 25 products |
| **Performance** | 7 SQL queries and reusable DAX measures powering automated KPI analysis |
| **Impact** | Tracked **₹29.6Cr revenue**, **36.7% profit margin**, and **15% YoY growth** |
| **Repository** | [Explore Repositories →](https://github.com/afsalahamed444-design?tab=repositories) |

**Engineering Overview**

Excel and Power Query cleaned and prepared 9,500+ sales transactions; PivotTables, SUMIFS, and COUNTIFS supported initial validation. Seven MySQL queries handled extraction, aggregation, and regional/product analysis. Power BI measures — including YoY Growth %, YTD Revenue, and RANKX — powered interactive reporting that identified East India as the lowest-performing region and informed a distributor-expansion recommendation.

**Key Findings**
- ₹29.6Cr total revenue analyzed across 25 products
- 36.7% overall profit margin, 15% YoY growth
- East India flagged as the lowest-performing region
- Distributor expansion proposed as a targeted growth strategy
